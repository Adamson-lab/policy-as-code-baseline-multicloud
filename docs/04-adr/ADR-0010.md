# ADR-0010: Break-glass principal excluded and alarmed

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Deciders:** Architecture board (author: Favour Adamson)

## Context
Emergencies need a way out of deny.

## Decision
`pac-platform-admin` is excluded via ArnNotLike, requires MFA, every use alarms and is reviewed in 2 business days.

## Alternatives considered
No bypass (outage risk); shared admin (no accountability).

## Consequences
A residual bypass exists by design (risk R-02).
