# Governance
Principles: one catalogue; audit before enforce; fidelity stated honestly; policy authors cannot deploy; exceptions expire; evidence by default.
Decision rights: new control or fidelity downgrade = ADR + board; severity critical enforcement = CISO sign-off; waivers = Security/GRC approves, CCoE operates (max 90 days, renewable once).
Cadence: policy board monthly; quarterly review of false positives and engine changes.
